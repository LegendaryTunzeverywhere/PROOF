# 🗣️ Languages (French) - Complete Curriculum (26 Lessons)

**Duration**: ~26 hours | **Level**: Beginner → Intermediate
**Outcome**: Hold real conversations in French

## Level 1: French Basics (9 lessons, 4 hours)
1. **Pronunciation** (25min) - Sounds, accents, silent letters
2. **Basic Greetings** (20min) - Hello, goodbye, please, thank you
3. **Personal Pronouns** (25min) - Je, tu, il/elle, nous, vous, ils/elles
4. **Present Tense -ER** (30min) - Regular verbs, conjugation
5. **Numbers 1-100** (25min) - Counting, telling time, dates
6. **Common Nouns** (25min) - Gender, articles (le/la/les)
7. **Basic Questions** (30min) - Who, what, where, when, why, how
8. **Negation** (25min) - Ne...pas, ne...jamais, ne...plus
9. **Introductions** (30min) - Name, age, nationality, profession

## Level 2: Practical Communication (9 lessons, 4.5 hours)
10. **Present Tense -IR/-RE** (30min) - Irregular patterns
11. **Food & Dining** (35min) - Ordering, preferences, vocabulary
12. **Shopping** (30min) - Asking prices, sizes, payments
13. **Directions** (30min) - Left/right, landmarks, asking for help
14. **Transportation** (30min) - Tickets, schedules, taxi/metro
15. **Weather & Seasons** (25min) - Describing weather, months
16. **Family & Relationships** (30min) - Family members, describing people
17. **Hobbies & Activities** (35min) - Likes/dislikes, free time
18. **Past Tense (Passé Composé)** (40min) - Formation, avoir/être

## Level 3: Fluent Conversations (8 lessons, 4.5 hours)
19. **Imperfect Tense** (35min) - Describing past, habits
20. **Future Tense** (35min) - Plans, predictions, intentions
21. **Conditional** (35min) - Would, politeness, hypotheticals
22. **Subjunctive Basics** (40min) - Necessity, emotion, doubt
23. **Comparisons** (30min) - More/less, superlatives
24. **Complex Sentences** (40min) - Relative pronouns, conjunctions
25. **Idioms & Expressions** (35min) - Common phrases, slang
26. **Cultural Nuances** (40min) - Formality, customs, etiquette

**Final Assessment**: 10-minute conversation (90min prep, 10 NIM, 500 XP)
