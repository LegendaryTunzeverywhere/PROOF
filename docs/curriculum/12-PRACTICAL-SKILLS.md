# 🔧 Practical Skills - Complete Curriculum (25 Lessons)

**Duration**: ~25 hours | **Level**: Beginner → Intermediate
**Outcome**: Manage everyday life competently

## Level 1: Personal Finance (9 lessons, 4 hours)
1. **Money Basics** (20min) - Income, expenses, cash flow
2. **Budgeting 101** (30min) - 50/30/20 rule, tracking, apps
3. **Banking** (25min) - Checking, savings, fees, online banking
4. **Credit Cards** (30min) - How they work, interest, building credit
5. **Debt Management** (30min) - Types, payoff strategies, avoiding
6. **Emergency Fund** (25min) - Why, how much, where to keep it
7. **Investing Basics** (35min) - Stocks, bonds, index funds, 401k
8. **Taxes** (30min) - Filing, deductions, withholding
9. **Insurance** (30min) - Health, auto, renters, life basics

## Level 2: Home & Health (8 lessons, 3.5 hours)
10. **Cooking Basics** (30min) - Kitchen tools, techniques, recipes
11. **Meal Planning** (25min) - Weekly planning, grocery lists, batch
12. **Nutrition** (25min) - Macros, portions, reading labels
13. **Cleaning Systems** (25min) - Daily, weekly, deep cleaning
14. **Basic Repairs** (30min) - Tools, fixing common issues
15. **Laundry** (20min) - Sorting, washing, drying, ironing
16. **Sleep Hygiene** (25min) - Routine, environment, habits
17. **Exercise** (30min) - Starting, consistency, types

## Level 3: Productivity & Planning (8 lessons, 4 hours)
18. **Time Management** (35min) - Priorities, calendars, batching
19. **Task Management** (30min) - To-do systems, tracking, completion
20. **Goal Setting** (35min) - SMART goals, tracking, accountability
21. **Habit Building** (35min) - Formation, stacking, maintaining
22. **Email Management** (25min) - Inbox zero, filters, unsubscribe
23. **File Organization** (30min) - Digital filing, naming, backups
24. **Decision Making** (35min) - Frameworks, avoiding paralysis
25. **Stress Management** (40min) - Techniques, boundaries, self-care

**Final Assessment**: 30-day life optimization plan (90min, 10 NIM, 500 XP)
