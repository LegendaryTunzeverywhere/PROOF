# ✍️ Writing - Complete Curriculum (23 Lessons)

**Duration**: ~23 hours | **Level**: Beginner → Intermediate
**Outcome**: Write clear, persuasive, engaging content

## Level 1: Writing Foundations (8 lessons, 3.5 hours)
1. **Clear Writing** (20min) - Simplicity, active voice, concision
2. **Sentence Structure** (25min) - Variety, rhythm, flow
3. **Paragraphs** (20min) - Topic sentences, unity, transitions
4. **Grammar Essentials** (30min) - Common errors, rules that matter
5. **Punctuation** (25min) - Commas, semicolons, dashes
6. **Word Choice** (25min) - Precise verbs, concrete nouns, avoiding jargon
7. **Tone & Voice** (25min) - Formal vs casual, consistency, personality
8. **Editing Basics** (30min) - Self-editing, revisions, proofreading

## Level 2: Content Writing (8 lessons, 4 hours)
9. **Headlines** (25min) - Attention, clarity, specificity
10. **Introductions** (30min) - Hooks, context, promises
11. **Body Structure** (30min) - Organization, subheadings, skimmability
12. **Conclusions** (25min) - Summary, CTA, lasting impression
13. **SEO Writing** (35min) - Keywords, structure, user intent
14. **Blog Posts** (35min) - Format, length, engagement
15. **Email Copy** (30min) - Subject lines, structure, conversion
16. **Landing Pages** (30min) - Value prop, benefits, objections

## Level 3: Persuasive Writing (7 lessons, 4 hours)
17. **Storytelling** (35min) - Arc, character, emotion, resolution
18. **Persuasion Techniques** (35min) - Ethos, pathos, logos
19. **Objection Handling** (30min) - Anticipating, addressing, reframing
20. **Social Proof** (30min) - Testimonials, case studies, numbers
21. **Copywriting Formulas** (35min) - AIDA, PAS, FAB
22. **Long-Form Content** (40min) - Structure, pacing, depth
23. **Portfolio Writing** (40min) - Case studies, showcasing work

**Final Assessment**: Write a complete landing page (90min, 10 NIM, 500 XP)
