# 📱 Social Media - Complete Curriculum (24 Lessons)

**Duration**: ~24 hours | **Level**: Beginner → Intermediate
**Outcome**: Build and grow social media presence

## Level 1: Social Media Basics (8 lessons, 3.5 hours)
1. **Platform Overview** (20min) - Major platforms, demographics, use cases
2. **Profile Optimization** (25min) - Bio, photo, links, consistency
3. **Content Pillars** (25min) - Topics, themes, mix
4. **Posting Basics** (25min) - Format, timing, frequency
5. **Captions** (30min) - Structure, hooks, CTAs, hashtags
6. **Visual Content** (25min) - Photos, graphics, video basics
7. **Engagement 101** (25min) - Comments, DMs, community building
8. **Analytics Basics** (30min) - Reach, engagement, growth metrics

## Level 2: Platform-Specific Strategy (8 lessons, 4 hours)
9. **Instagram Strategy** (35min) - Feed, Stories, Reels, algorithm
10. **Twitter/X Strategy** (30min) - Threads, timing, engagement
11. **LinkedIn Strategy** (35min) - Professional content, networking
12. **TikTok Strategy** (35min) - Short-form video, trends, algorithm
13. **Facebook Strategy** (30min) - Groups, Pages, community
14. **YouTube Basics** (35min) - Thumbnails, titles, descriptions
15. **Pinterest Strategy** (25min) - Pins, boards, SEO
16. **Platform Selection** (30min) - Where your audience is, focus

## Level 3: Growth & Monetization (8 lessons, 4.5 hours)
17. **Content Calendar** (35min) - Planning, batching, scheduling
18. **Viral Content** (35min) - What makes content spread, timing
19. **Paid Advertising** (40min) - Ad types, targeting, budgets
20. **Influencer Outreach** (30min) - Finding, pitching, collaborating
21. **Community Management** (35min) - Moderation, engagement, crisis
22. **Analytics Deep-Dive** (40min) - Insights, optimization, reporting
23. **Monetization** (35min) - Sponsorships, products, courses
24. **Automation Tools** (40min) - Scheduling, analytics, management

**Final Assessment**: 30-day social media strategy (90min, 10 NIM, 500 XP)
