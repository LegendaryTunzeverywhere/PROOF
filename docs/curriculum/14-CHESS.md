# ♟️ Chess Curriculum

**Category:** Games & Strategy  
**Difficulty:** Beginner to Intermediate  
**Total Topics:** 22  
**Estimated Time:** ~11-13 hours  
**Total Rewards:** ~95 NIM, ~3500 XP

## Overview

The Chess curriculum provides a comprehensive learning path from absolute beginner to intermediate tournament player. Students learn through structured lessons, tactical puzzles, position analysis, and interactive challenges using the integrated Chess King components with AI coaching.

## Learning Objectives

By completing this curriculum, students will:

- ✅ Master fundamental chess rules, piece movement, and notation
- ✅ Recognize and execute tactical patterns (pins, forks, skewers, discoveries)
- ✅ Understand opening principles and play standard opening systems
- ✅ Apply middlegame strategic concepts (pawn structure, piece coordination, king safety)
- ✅ Win basic endgames with proper technique
- ✅ Analyze games systematically to identify and fix weaknesses
- ✅ Compete effectively with practical tournament skills

## Curriculum Structure

### Level 1: Fundamentals (7 Topics, Difficulty: 1)
**Foundation for all chess knowledge**

1. **Board Basics & Piece Movement** (30 min)
   - Chessboard setup and coordinate system
   - How each piece moves
   - Interactive board setup challenge

2. **Special Moves & Game Rules** (30 min)
   - Castling, en passant, promotion
   - Check, checkmate, stalemate
   - Special moves practice

3. **Chess Notation & Recording Games** (25 min)
   - Algebraic notation system
   - Reading and writing moves
   - Notation puzzle challenge

4. **Fundamental Tactics: Pin, Fork, Skewer** (35 min)
   - Three core tactical patterns
   - Recognition and execution
   - 6 tactical puzzles to solve

5. **Opening Principles** (30 min)
   - Control the center
   - Develop pieces quickly
   - King safety through castling
   - Opening play scenarios

6. **Basic Endgames: K+Q vs K, K+R vs K** (30 min)
   - Checkmate techniques
   - Opposition and stalemate awareness
   - Endgame practice positions

7. **Chess Thinking Process** (30 min)
   - Systematic decision-making
   - Candidate moves and calculation
   - Blunder prevention routine
   - Position analysis exercises

**Level 1 Totals:** ~210 minutes, 20 NIM, 910 XP

---

### Level 2: Beginner (8 Topics, Difficulty: 2)
**Building tactical and positional skills**

8. **Tactical Motifs: Discovery, Deflection, Decoy** (35 min)
   - Advanced tactical themes
   - Combination calculation
   - 6 advanced puzzles

9. **Opening Systems: 1.e4 (Open Games)** (35 min)
   - Italian Game
   - Ruy Lopez (Spanish)
   - Sicilian Defense
   - Opening repertoire building

10. **Opening Systems: 1.d4 (Closed Games)** (35 min)
    - Queen's Gambit
    - London System
    - Indian Defenses
    - Positional opening play

11. **Pawn Structure & Weaknesses** (30 min)
    - Isolated, doubled, backward pawns
    - Passed pawns
    - Structure evaluation exercises

12. **King Safety & Attacking the King** (30 min)
    - Pawn shield maintenance
    - Greek Gift sacrifice
    - Rook lifts and pawn storms
    - Attack and defense puzzles

13. **Piece Coordination & Harmony** (30 min)
    - Doubled rooks
    - Queen-bishop batteries
    - Knight outposts
    - Coordination improvement drills

14. **Elementary Endgames: Pawn Endings** (30 min)
    - Opposition technique
    - Square rule
    - Pawn races
    - Key square concepts

15. **Blunder Prevention & Error Reduction** (25 min)
    - Systematic blunder-checking
    - Common mistake types
    - Recovery techniques
    - Blunder avoidance exercises

**Level 2 Totals:** ~250 minutes, 29 NIM, 1200 XP

---

### Level 3: Intermediate (7 Topics, Difficulty: 3)
**Advanced tactics, strategy, and practical play**

16. **Advanced Tactical Patterns** (35 min)
    - Removal of defender
    - Interference and X-ray
    - Windmill and zwischenzug
    - 7 complex combination puzzles

17. **Middlegame Strategy & Planning** (35 min)
    - Strategic planning from position features
    - Understanding imbalances
    - Prophylactic thinking
    - Strategic position analysis

18. **Attack & Defense Balance** (30 min)
    - Calculating attacks (attackers vs defenders)
    - Defensive techniques
    - Dynamic balance in mutual attacks
    - Attack/defense scenarios

19. **Positional Play & Long-term Advantages** (35 min)
    - Space, weak squares, bad pieces
    - Improving piece placement
    - Accumulating small advantages
    - Positional evaluation exercises

20. **Complex Endgames: Rook & Minor Piece** (35 min)
    - Rook endgame principles (Lucena, Philidor)
    - Rook vs minor piece
    - Bishop vs knight endings
    - Technical endgame positions

21. **Game Analysis & Improvement** (30 min)
    - Systematic analysis process
    - Finding and understanding mistakes
    - Creating personalized study plans
    - Master game analysis

22. **Tournament Skills & Practical Play** (30 min)
    - Time management
    - Psychological factors
    - Practical decision-making
    - Tournament simulation games

**Level 3 Totals:** ~230 minutes, 37 NIM, 1190 XP

## Integration with Chess King

The curriculum is designed to integrate with the [Chess King](https://github.com/Iamsdt/chess) open-source chess application, providing:

### Interactive Features
- **Live Chess Board:** Interactive board for setup and move practice
- **Puzzle Solver:** Tactical puzzle interface with hint system
- **Position Analyzer:** Visual position evaluation with piece highlighting
- **Opening Explorer:** Opening move tree with transpositions
- **Endgame Trainer:** Structured endgame practice positions
- **Game Analyzer:** Move-by-move analysis with engine evaluation

### AI Coaching Integration
- **Move Suggestions:** AI suggests candidate moves with explanations
- **Mistake Detection:** Real-time blunder warnings
- **Pattern Recognition:** Highlights tactical motifs in positions
- **Personalized Feedback:** Adapts explanations to student level
- **Progress Tracking:** Monitors improvement in tactical vision

### Technical Implementation
Each challenge includes:
```javascript
{
  type: 'chess',
  kind: 'puzzle' | 'opening' | 'endgame' | 'analysis' | etc.,
  // Challenge-specific configuration
  evaluator: {
    type: 'chess',
    config: {
      mode: 'puzzle',
      puzzleCount: 6,
      exactMoves: true,
      stockfishVerify: true
    }
  }
}
```

## Teaching Methodology

### Progressive Difficulty
Topics build systematically:
1. **Rules & Mechanics** → Move pieces correctly
2. **Basic Tactics** → Win material
3. **Opening Principles** → Start games well
4. **Strategic Concepts** → Understand positions
5. **Advanced Tactics** → Find complex combinations
6. **Endgame Technique** → Convert advantages
7. **Practical Skills** → Compete effectively

### Active Learning Components

**Every Topic Includes:**
- **TL;DR:** Quick overview for context
- **Lesson Sections:** 3 focused teaching segments
- **Example:** Concrete demonstration with positions
- **Reflective Question:** Prompts self-assessment
- **Key Points:** 5-7 essential takeaways
- **Misconception:** Common error to avoid
- **Practice (2):** Low-stakes practice with hints
- **Quiz (3):** Knowledge verification
- **Challenge:** Applied skill demonstration

### Socratic Elements
- Questions prompt active thinking before revealing answers
- Hints guide discovery without giving solutions
- "Why" explanations deepen understanding
- Misconceptions address common errors proactively

### Practical Application
Every lesson connects to:
- Real game situations
- Concrete positions to analyze
- Practical decision-making
- Tournament-relevant skills

## Learning Path Recommendations

### For Absolute Beginners
1. Complete Level 1 topics in order (1-7)
2. Practice tactics daily with puzzles
3. Play slow games (15+ minutes)
4. Analyze every game afterward
5. Move to Level 2 when confident

### For Casual Players (Know Rules)
1. Review Level 1 topics 4-7 (tactics, openings, endgames, thinking)
2. Complete all Level 2 topics
3. Focus on topics 11, 14, 15 (structure, endgames, blunders)
4. Play regular games with time controls
5. Progress to Level 3

### For Intermediate Players (1000+ rating)
1. Skim Level 1 and 2 for gaps
2. Focus on Level 3 topics (16-22)
3. Emphasize topics 17, 19, 21 (strategy, positional play, analysis)
4. Study master games
5. Complete final assessment

### For Tournament Preparation
**Priority Topics:**
- Topic 7: Chess Thinking Process
- Topic 15: Blunder Prevention
- Topic 18: Attack & Defense Balance
- Topic 21: Game Analysis
- Topic 22: Tournament Skills

1. **Tactical Vision:** 8/10 puzzles solved correctly
2. **Opening Knowledge:** 3 systems played accurately

**Pass Score:** 75% overall (demonstrates competency)

```javascript
  type: 'chess',

    puzzleCount?: number,

    maxMoves?: number,

  }
}
```

**FEN Positions:**
Positions specified in standard FEN (Forsyth-Edwards Notation):
```
rnbqkbnr/pppppppp/8/8/8/8/PPPPPPPP/RNBQKBNR w KQkq - 0 1
```

- `server/ai/kb.js` - Live curriculum content and skill entry
