# 📈 Marketing - Complete Curriculum (23 Lessons)

**Duration**: ~23 hours | **Level**: Beginner → Intermediate
**Outcome**: Plan and execute effective marketing campaigns

## Level 1: Marketing Foundations (8 lessons, 3.5 hours)
1. **What is Marketing?** (20min) - Definition, misconceptions, scope
2. **Target Audience** (25min) - Demographics, psychographics, personas
3. **Customer Journey** (25min) - Awareness, consideration, decision
4. **Value Proposition** (25min) - Features vs benefits, unique value
5. **Positioning** (30min) - Market position, competitive advantage
6. **Brand Basics** (25min) - Voice, personality, consistency
7. **Marketing Channels** (25min) - Paid, owned, earned media
8. **Metrics Basics** (30min) - KPIs, ROI, attribution basics

## Level 2: Content & Messaging (8 lessons, 4 hours)
9. **Copywriting Basics** (25min) - Headlines, body, CTAs
10. **Storytelling** (30min) - Narrative structure, emotion, memory
11. **Landing Pages** (35min) - Structure, conversion elements, testing
12. **Email Marketing** (30min) - Subject lines, structure, automation
13. **Social Media Copy** (30min) - Platform-specific writing, engagement
14. **SEO Fundamentals** (35min) - Keywords, on-page, link building
15. **Content Calendar** (30min) - Planning, batching, consistency
16. **A/B Testing** (30min) - Hypothesis, variants, analysis

## Level 3: Campaigns & Strategy (7 lessons, 4 hours)
17. **Campaign Planning** (35min) - Objectives, strategy, tactics
18. **Paid Advertising** (40min) - Platforms, targeting, budgeting
19. **Social Media Strategy** (35min) - Platform selection, posting strategy
20. **Influencer Marketing** (30min) - Finding, outreach, partnerships
21. **Launch Strategy** (40min) - Pre-launch, launch, post-launch
22. **Growth Tactics** (35min) - Referrals, partnerships, PR
23. **Analytics & Optimization** (40min) - Google Analytics, reporting, iteration

**Final Assessment**: Complete campaign plan (90min, 10 NIM, 500 XP)
