## 📚 Lesson Structure (Every Lesson)

Each of the 287 lessons includes:

1. **Duration** - Time estimate (20-45 minutes)
2. **Goal** - What you'll be able to do
3. **Content Sections** - 3-4 focused teaching blocks
4. **TL;DR** - One-sentence takeaway
5. **Examples** - Working code/designs/copy
6. **Practice Questions** - 2-3 with hints
7. **Key Points** - 4 bullet-point summary
8. **Common Misconceptions** - What people get wrong
9. **Challenge** - Hands-on proof (20-45 min)
10. **Rubric** - Server-side evaluation criteria

## 📝 Content Guidelines

Every lesson must have:
1. **Clear learning objective** (one sentence)
2. **Step-by-step instruction** (3-4 sections)
3. **Working examples** (copy-paste ready)
4. **Practice questions** (with explanations)
5. **Real challenge** (portfolio-worthy)
6. **Rubric criteria** (objective grading)

Every challenge must:
1. **Build something real** (not toy problems)
2. **Take 20-45 minutes** (not hours)
3. **Be hand-typed** (no paste)
4. **Have clear requirements** (3-5 items)
5. **Grade objectively** (rubric-based)
6. **Reward completion** (NIM + XP)
