# 💼 Business - Complete Curriculum (25 Lessons)

**Duration**: ~25 hours | **Level**: Beginner → Intermediate
**Outcome**: Launch and run a viable business

## Level 1: Business Fundamentals (8 lessons, 3.5 hours)
1. **Business Basics** (20min) - Types, structures, fundamentals
2. **Idea Validation** (25min) - Problem, solution, market fit
3. **Business Models** (30min) - Revenue streams, cost structure
4. **Target Market** (25min) - Segmentation, personas, TAM/SAM/SOM
5. **Competitive Analysis** (25min) - Competitors, positioning, differentiation
6. **Value Proposition** (25min) - Unique value, positioning statement
7. **Business Planning** (30min) - Lean canvas, business plan basics
8. **Legal Basics** (25min) - Registration, licenses, liability

## Level 2: Operations & Finance (9 lessons, 4.5 hours)
9. **Pricing Strategy** (30min) - Cost-plus, value-based, competitive
10. **Revenue Models** (30min) - One-time, recurring, freemium, tiered
11. **Financial Basics** (35min) - Revenue, expenses, profit, cash flow
12. **Budgeting** (30min) - Creating budgets, tracking, forecasting
13. **Accounting 101** (35min) - Bookkeeping, taxes, statements
14. **Funding Options** (35min) - Bootstrapping, loans, investors
15. **Hiring Basics** (30min) - Contractors, employees, process
16. **Tools & Systems** (30min) - Software, automation, efficiency
17. **Customer Service** (35min) - Support, retention, satisfaction

## Level 3: Growth & Scaling (8 lessons, 4.5 hours)
18. **Sales Funnel** (35min) - Awareness, consideration, conversion
19. **Sales Techniques** (40min) - Prospecting, pitching, closing
20. **Customer Acquisition** (35min) - Channels, CAC, LTV
21. **Product Development** (40min) - MVP, iteration, roadmap
22. **Partnerships** (30min) - Finding, negotiating, managing
23. **Metrics & KPIs** (40min) - What to track, dashboards, decisions
24. **Scaling Strategies** (40min) - Systems, delegation, growth
25. **Exit Planning** (35min) - Acquisition, IPO, succession

**Final Assessment**: Complete business plan (90min, 10 NIM, 500 XP)
