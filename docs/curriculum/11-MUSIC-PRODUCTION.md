# 🎵 Music Production - Complete Curriculum (24 Lessons)

**Duration**: ~24 hours | **Level**: Beginner → Intermediate
**Outcome**: Produce complete tracks from scratch

## Level 1: Music Fundamentals (8 lessons, 3.5 hours)
1. **Sound Basics** (20min) - Frequency, amplitude, waveforms
2. **Music Theory** (30min) - Notes, scales, intervals
3. **Rhythm & Tempo** (25min) - BPM, time signatures, groove
4. **Chords** (30min) - Major, minor, progressions
5. **Melody** (25min) - Writing melodies, motifs, hooks
6. **Song Structure** (25min) - Intro, verse, chorus, bridge
7. **Listening Skills** (25min) - Active listening, reference tracks
8. **DAW Basics** (30min) - Interface, tracks, recording

## Level 2: Production Techniques (8 lessons, 4 hours)
9. **MIDI Basics** (30min) - Piano roll, quantization, editing
10. **Audio Recording** (30min) - Microphones, levels, monitoring
11. **Drums & Percussion** (35min) - Patterns, programming, samples
12. **Bass Lines** (30min) - Writing bass, synth vs real
13. **Instruments** (35min) - Virtual instruments, layering
14. **Sampling** (30min) - Finding, chopping, pitching samples
15. **Automation** (30min) - Volume, panning, effects over time
16. **Arrangement** (35min) - Building energy, transitions, drops

## Level 3: Mixing & Mastering (8 lessons, 4.5 hours)
17. **Mixing Basics** (35min) - Levels, panning, balance
18. **EQ** (35min) - Frequency spectrum, cutting, boosting
19. **Compression** (40min) - Dynamics, ratio, attack, release
20. **Reverb & Delay** (35min) - Space, depth, atmosphere
21. **Effects Chain** (30min) - Order, processing, creativity
22. **Mixing Workflow** (40min) - Start to finish, reference
23. **Mastering Basics** (40min) - Loudness, limiting, final polish
24. **Export & Distribution** (30min) - Formats, metadata, platforms

**Final Assessment**: Produce a complete track (120min, 12 NIM, 600 XP)
